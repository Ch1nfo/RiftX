# Scope and Safety References

- Scope is defined by the durable Run, including exclusions and time bounds.
- Approval belongs to one exact Tool Call Intent and does not silently transfer to changed arguments.
- RunKind Effect Policy is enforced by the owning application service, not by Skill text.
- Browser observations, execution claims, and resource owners are versioned and must be current.
- Terminal Run status requires affirmative physical-stop evidence for all managed effect families.
